# QTIP2 Backpack Campaign: 24-Hour Chronicle (2026-07-27–28)

Public operator identity: **banana_bae**

This is the public, receipt-bound account of the Wire-C campaign from tier acquisition through the next exact TRUE-C measurement chain. It separates physical measurements from priced projections, estimates, diagnostics, and still-pending work.

## Executive result

The campaign did not validate the original expectation that the first physical Wire-C build would be a large improvement. On the exact BALANCED64 instrument:

- Genesis/BQ3 control: **0.1293130 KLD** measured.
- Wire A pre-repair: **0.1159266 KLD** measured.
- Wire C-R pre-repair: **0.1181381 KLD** measured. “C-R” means the physical wire used substituted frozen-base codebooks for 3,803 intended TRUE-C VQ cells.
- P922 measured the substitution surcharge as **0.0292596 KLD** (**2.92596 global points**), far larger than preregistration.
- Therefore TRUE-C is currently an **estimate**, approximately **0.089–0.095 KLD**, until direct pre/post measurements seal. The arithmetic point estimate from C-R minus the P922 surcharge is 0.0888785; the published interval is an uncertainty/transfer allowance, not a measured confidence interval.

The corrected P930 calibration surface now retrodicts all four global anchors within 5% relative error, but six class-level misses remain disclosed. A new direct chain is preregistered to replace the estimate with physical measurements.

This publication integration was CPU/text-only: it copied and scrubbed receipts, recomputed
tables, verified hashes, and prepared Git artifacts. It makes no new GPU execution claim.

## What happened, in order

### 1. Exact QTIP3 and QTIP2 acquisition

QTIP3 and QTIP2 were acquired as exact per-cell tier surfaces under a fixed 43-layer × 512-cell model geometry. Every accepted cell carried a source identity, payload size, SHA-256, pack/runtime metadata, and layer-seal receipt. Missing or quarantined identities failed closed rather than being silently substituted.

The public package includes the exact QTIP3 and QTIP2 BALANCED64 anchor receipts plus a frozen acquisition-spec inventory. It does not include model weights or the approximately terabyte-scale tensor payloads.

### 2. Local staging and the bulk-transfer lesson

Tensor payloads were staged to compute-local disk before GPU work. The campaign used the QSFP fabric for payload transport and reserved management links for control traffic. A representative base stage moved about 95 GB end-to-end in about 10 minutes, including hashing/extraction; that 0.158 GB/s end-to-end figure is not a clean link benchmark.

Measured and operational transfer receipts:

| receipt | payload/shape | elapsed | observed or gate | interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| direct fabric probe | 30 GB contiguous | <6 s | >5.0 GB/s | clean host-to-host capability |
| 8-way directory transfer | large tree | campaign observation | ~2 GB/s | default bulk recipe |
| 95 GB base stage | archive + verify + extract | ~600 s | ~0.158 GB/s end-to-end | includes non-network work; not a fabric limit |
| 4-way tar+TCP | very large tar sets | policy gate | ≥2 GB/s required | use when directory rsync shape is unsuitable |

Single-stream transfers above 10 GB are prohibited. Slow bulk transfer is treated as a tooling/configuration defect to repair, not a reason to fall back to NAS.

### 3. Corrected pricing and the retrodiction failure

The first corrected price grid closed globally but failed the binding Wire-B retrodiction by class. The most important miss was CODE: measured Wire-B change was +23.3%, while the grid retrodicted only +1.03744% (a 22.26256 percentage-point miss). CHAT also exceeded the 10-point absolute-error gate.

That gate correctly said “stop.” An explicit operator override authorized only a prominently labeled baseline preview solve so the system could be observed without pretending the model was validated. This distinction matters: a preview under a failed model gate is an experiment, not a production recommendation.

### 4. Baseline solve: why greedy-zero did not mean optimal

The first legal warm start met the exact byte envelope but made zero purchases. A one-cell gain-per-byte greedy pass also found zero feasible improving moves. That negative result was initially misleading: exact byte coupling made the useful moves pairwise rather than individually feasible.

A full 286k-binary SCIP run spent more than 800 seconds without improving the warm start. Dominance pruning removed 17,365 options, but a longer primal-heavy run still failed to surface the good incumbent.

The recovery was an LP-basic candidate plus an exact tiny repair: only two fractional groups remained, and a 28-variable/9-constraint repair solved in 3 ms. It produced a replay-verified preview assignment with:

- priced objective 0.0299040206;
- 101,346,585,857 bytes, 114,554 bytes slack;
- 21,474 changed cells;
- 14,973 QTIP3 and 2,268 QTIP2 purchases;
- rigorous LP relative gap 1.33367e-5, with no optimality claim.

The reusable solver lesson is the **paired-swap rescue**: when no one-cell move is feasible, pair a freeing downgrade with a beneficial upgrade, evaluate the pair atomically against byte and class constraints, and use a deterministic identity/tier tie-break. “Greedy found zero” is a diagnostic, not a proof.

### 5. Three-host physical overlay build and the volatile-scratch loss

The physical build initially depended too heavily on volatile scratch state. A blocked run lost its workspace, and four upper payload paths were discovered to be shm-only. At the same time, overlapping recovery lanes crossed layer boundaries before stop signals took effect, producing duplicate but hash-verifiable work.

The recovery used three disjoint compute hosts under one fan-in contract:

- disk-direct per-cell output and fsynced every-64 checkpoints;
- per-layer deterministic seals and full rereads;
- first-complete-layer-wins arbitration only at atomic boundaries;
- exact process/start-time ownership for stop/release;
- durable peer copies before retiring a producer;
- a terminal 43-layer completeness ledger.

The final ledger covered 21,472 changed cells plus 544 immutable inherited cells = 22,016/22,016
assignment cells, with zero gaps and zero volatile-scratch payload dependency. A pre-release
comparison also caught that one lower-layer handoff belonged to the older preview assignment;
the layer was rebuilt against the final assignment before release. Duplicate or stale layers
were explicitly excluded rather than blended.

### 6. Pre-repair BALANCED64 measurement

P921 measured the physical Wire-A comparator and Wire C-R on exactly the same BALANCED64 instrument:

- Wire A: 0.1159265977 KLD.
- Wire C-R: 0.1181380905 KLD.
- A−C paired mean: −0.0022114928 KLD, 95% CI [−0.01212694, 0.00770395].

Wire C-R was numerically worse than A, but the global paired interval crossed zero. The result is operationally disappointing, not globally separated at 95%.

### 7. P922 isolated the substitution penalty

P922 restored the exact 3,803 intended VQ identities while retaining the frozen base codebooks, then measured the result on the same instrument. The diagnostic candidate measured 0.1466261360 KLD. Relative to the same cells’ priced TRUE-C expectation, the substitution surcharge was:

- 0.0292596322 KLD;
- 2.9259632 global points;
- 95% CI [0.02357005, 0.03494922] KLD.

This is a material codebook-substitution effect. The P922 diagnostic is **not** a direct TRUE-C measurement and must not be relabeled as one.

## Reusable operating laws

1. Stage payloads locally before compute; NAS is archive/backup only.
2. Hash source, staged, consumed, and produced bytes independently.
3. Use disk-direct, fsynced checkpoints; shm is cache, never sole authority.
4. A blocked card never owns a live payload: exact-release or immediately hand off to one live successor.
5. Adopt orphaned detached work only after exact PID/start-time, claim, log, and checkpoint verification.
6. Speculative parallelism needs disjoint atomic partitions, first-seal-wins rules, and bounded stop points before launch.
7. A solver gate failure may only be overridden as a clearly labeled preview.
8. One-cell greedy failure does not rule out improving paired swaps.
9. Compare wires only on the same frozen instrument, teacher/model lineage, reducer, support, and window manifest.
10. Separate measured values, projections, estimates, and pending results in every table.

### Asymmetric infrastructure layout

- **Mac control plane:** Kanban, orchestration, public scrubbing, verification, and Git publication.
- **Compute hosts:** exact-claim solving, physical build/readback, and measurement.
- **QSFP fabric:** peer transfer of immutable objects; throughput is measured, never inferred from line rate.
- **Archive storage:** cold source and durable receipts, not a mid-layer runtime filesystem.
- **Persistent local disks:** active hot set, checkpoints, manifests, and restart authority.

The asymmetry is deliberate: control, archive, transfer, hot staging, and compute have different
failure modes and should not be collapsed into a nominally symmetric shared-filesystem design.

### Bulk-transfer recipe

```bash
python3 code/bulk_transfer.py \
  --inventory inventory.tsv \
  --source-root "$SOURCE_ROOT" \
  --dest-root "$DEST_ROOT" \
  --jobs 8 \
  --min-free-bytes 17179869184 \
  --receipt transfer_receipt.json
```

The helper capacity-gates the whole batch, hashes source and destination, writes temporary files,
fsyncs, atomically renames, and records measured per-object and aggregate throughput. Eight jobs
at roughly 2 GB/s was a campaign operating point, not a universal guarantee.

### Incomplete artifacts and speculative computation

Partial work is valid only when its scope is in the label (`39_OF_40`, exact layer range, or exact
identity set), omissions are enumerated, and source/public hashes are bound. Speculative lanes
must be disjoint before launch, stop only at atomic boundaries, and obey first-seal-wins fan-in.
A blocked or completed card cannot remain a writer. Detached work is adopted only after exact
claim, PID/start-time, log, and checkpoint verification; otherwise it is terminated and replaced
by one live owner.
